2021-2022学年“优秀外籍及港澳台”评选结果公示

Announcement on “Outstanding International Teachers” For Academic Year 2021-2022

根据《广东外语外贸大学“优秀外籍及港澳台教师”评选办法》，在各教学单位评选、推荐的基础上，经国际处审核，拟评选Allan Geere等17位教师为2021-2022学年度优秀外籍及港澳台教师，现将名单予以公示（详见附件）。

In accordance with the Rules of Guangdong University of Assessment Criteria for Selecting “Outstanding International Teachers” and based on the recommendations from the faculties/schools and the review by the International Office, the University will select 17 teachers as the “Outstanding International Teachers” for Academic Year 2021-2022 and the list is now announced (see attached name list).

公示期间，如有不同意见，请通过书面向国际处反映。反映情况时要签署真实姓名，要有具体事实。不签署真实姓名以及不提供具体事实材料的，一律不予受理。

If you have any different opinions about the nomination, please write to International Office before July 1. The objection should be reflected with specified facts signed in real name. Objection without real name or specific factual materials shall not be accepted.

公示时间Period：2022.6.29-2022.7.1
