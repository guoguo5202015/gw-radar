2020-2021学年度“优秀境外教师”评选结果公示

Outstanding International Teachers Academic Year2020-2021

根据《广东外语外贸大学“优秀境外教师”评选办法》，在各教学单位评选、推荐的基础上，经国际处审核，拟评选Anastasia Gkortsila等15位教师为2020-2021学年度优秀境外教师，现将名单予以公示（详见附件）。

In accordance with the Assessment Criteria for Selecting Outstanding International Teachers and based on the recommendations from the faculties/schools and the review by the International Office, the University will select 15 teachers as the Outstanding International Teachers for Academic Year 2020-2021 (please see the attachment for the candidates list).

公示期间，如有不同意见，请通过电话或书面向国际处反映。反映情况时要自报或签署真实姓名，要有具体事实。不报或不签署真实姓名以及不提供具体事实材料的，一律不予受理。

If anyone has any different opinions about the nomination, please contact International Office before July 16. The complaint must be true and supported by evidence.
