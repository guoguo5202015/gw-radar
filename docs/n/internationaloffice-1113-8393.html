2019-2020 学年度优秀境外教师评选结果公示

根据《广东外语外贸大学优秀境外教师评选办法》，学校组织了2019-2020学年度优秀境外教师评选工作。在各教学单位评选推荐的基础上，经国际处审核，决定推选东方语言文化学院Bae,Yunkyung等20位教师为2019-2020学年度优秀境外教师，现予以公示（名单见附件）。

公示期间，如有不同意见，请电话或书面向国际处反映。反映情况时要自报或签署真实姓名，要有具体事实。

公示时间：2020年8月11日至13日

Outstanding International Teachers Academic Year 2019-2020

In accordance with the Assessment Criteria for Selecting Outstanding International Teachers and based on the recommendations from the faculties/schools and the review by the International Office, the University decides to select Bae,Yunkyung and other 20 teachers as the Outstanding International Teachers for Academic Year 2019-2020 (please see the attachment for the candidates list).

If anyone has any disagreement about the nomination, please contact International Office before August 14. The complaint must be true and supported by evidence.
